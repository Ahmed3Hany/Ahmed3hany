My story

Driven by curiosity.
Focused on craft.

I'm an IT student at Borg Al Arab Technological University with a passion for coding since I was 15 years old.

I started with Scratch, moved on to Frontend Development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap), and explored Desktop Applications using Visual Basic, C#, and SQL Server.

Along the way, I also explored Electronics and Robotics (Lego EV3, Arduino), and even tried Mobile Development with Flutter.

After exploring many paths, I found my true focus — creating responsive, user-friendly websites. This portfolio is one of my proudest projects, built entirely by me using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap.
